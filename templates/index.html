Why Not Rely Solely on Accuracy?

Because 99.83% of all transactions in this dataset are legitimate, a naive "dummy" model that predicts every single transaction is legitimate would achieve an impressive 99.83% accuracy while failing to catch any fraud!

In financial fraud detection, Recall (Sensitivity) is paramount because failing to catch a fraud (False Negative) results in direct financial loss. Meanwhile, Precision ensures legitimate users are not inundated with false card declines.

Why Random Forest over Logistic Regression?

While Logistic Regression achieved a high Recall (91.84%), its Precision was only 6.10%, generating 1,386 false alarms on the test set.

The Random Forest Classifier achieved a much superior balance: 82.65% Recall with 71.68% Precision, cutting false alarms down to only 32 and yielding an F1-Score of 0.7678 (compared to 0.1144).
